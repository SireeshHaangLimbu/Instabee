Data and method
  • Lockers: OpenStreetMap (snapshot 2026-10-20). It maps 377 Instabee lockers, only part of the real network, so Instabee coverage here is a lower bound.
  • Residents: SCB 1 km population grid 2025, spread over Overture building footprints into ~175 m hexagons. Areas: SCB DeSO 2025.
  • Host sites: Overture Places 2026-09-23 (supermarkets, petrol stations, pharmacies, convenience stores, malls).
  • Parcel volumes and seasonality: Paketindex Q2 2026, Transportföretagen.
  • Placement uses greedy maximal covering on uncovered, age-weighted residents within a straight-line radius. Demand figures are estimates.
